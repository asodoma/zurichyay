Public transport in Zurich is expensive. Good news: the city is compact, and a 24-hour ticket is the best deal.

Public transport tickets (ZVV)

One ticket covers trams, buses, S-Bahn trains and the Limmat boats within the city (zone 110). Prices below are for adults, 2nd class.

TicketCHFEURCZK
Short-distance ticketfor a few stops only, check the ZVV app for the areaCHF 2.80€ 2.9973 CZK
Single ticketvalid for 1 hourCHF 4.70€ 5.02123 CZK
24-hour ticketunlimited rides, best valueCHF 9.40€ 10.04245 CZK

A 24-hour ticket costs exactly as much as two single tickets – so it already pays off with your third ride of the day. Prices valid from 14 December 2025, see zvv.ch. Converted with the ECB reference rate of 6 October 2026 (1 CHF ≈ 1.07 EUR ≈ 26.1 CZK).
